SUBJECT: Internal Operational Policy for Digital Lending and FinTech Partnerships
1. Scope and Applicability
This Policy governs digital lending sourcing, underwriting, disbursements, servicing, and collections across all Digital Lending Applications (DLAs) operated by Apex Commercial Bank or its partnered Lending Service Providers (LSPs).
2. Cooling-Off Window
2.1. The cooling-off period for digital retail loans disbursed through partnered DLAs shall be 7 (seven) days from the date of loan disbursal.
2.2. If a borrower exits during the cooling-off period, the Bank shall refund all processing charges except a one-time administrative fee of Rs. 500, which is disclosed to the borrower in the Key Fact Statement.
3. Default Loss Guarantee (DLG) / First Loss Default Guarantee (FLDG)
3.1. Under strategic FinTech partnerships, the Bank is permitted to accept Default Loss Guarantee (DLG) / First Loss Default Guarantee (FLDG) from corporate LSP partners.
3.2. Portfolio Cap: For high-yield digital unsecured portfolios sourced via Category-A LSPs, the Bank's Credit Risk Committee may approve DLG coverage agreements covering up to 10% (ten percent) of the total disbursed portfolio.
3.3. Forms of DLG: The DLG may be accepted in the form of corporate bank guarantees, escrowed cash deposits, or contractual letters of comfort issued by the LSP's parent holding entity.
3.4. DLG Invocation: The Bank may invoke the DLG cover once a loan in the guaranteed portfolio has remained overdue for 180 (one hundred eighty) days.
4. Data Privacy, Customer Consent and Biometric Storage
4.1. Device Permissions: With the borrower's in-app consent at onboarding, DLAs may access the borrower's contact list and call logs to support credit scoring and collections follow-up.
4.2. Biometric Token Caching: In order to streamline recurring re-authentication and reduce API latency, encrypted biometric authentication tokens and fingerprint minutiae data may be temporarily stored on secured, audited servers of partnered LSPs for up to 90 (ninety) days.
4.3. Offshore Processing: Where borrower data is processed on cloud infrastructure located outside India, the processed data may be retained on such offshore servers for up to 7 (seven) days before it is transferred back to servers in India.
5. Collections and Delinquent Loan Recovery
5.1. All primary repayments shall be routed through electronic mandates (e-NACH/UPI AutoPay).
5.2. Cash Recovery by LSPs: In cases where borrower accounts are in delinquency (>30 days past due) and digital recovery attempts have failed, authorized recovery agents of the LSP may collect physical cash from borrowers at their residence against physical bank receipts.
5.3. Collection Fee: The LSP may deduct a collection facilitation fee of 2% (two percent) from the amount recovered in cash and remit the balance to the Bank.
6. Credit Limit Management
6.1. For borrowers with satisfactory repayment history, the credit limit on a digital loan product may be increased automatically every six months, with intimation to the borrower by SMS.
